**Question 2**
---
### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? 

According to Roger Fisher, the key points of Reflective Listening are:

* Listen carefully and give full attention to the speaker.
* Understand both the words and emotions of the other person.
* Repeat or explain the message in your own words to confirm understanding.
* Use body language, eye contact, and expressions to show interest.
* Verify the information so that there are no misunderstandings.
* Take notes during important discussions or meetings.
* Share and confirm the discussed points with everyone involved.
* Make sure all people are on the same page before taking action.
* Stay calm and respectful, especially during emotional conversations.
* Focus on clear communication to avoid mistakes in work or relationships.

**Question 3**
---
### What are the obstacles in your listening process?
Some common obstacles in my listening process are
* Thinking about my reply instead of   listening carefully.
* Lack of concentration during long conversations or meetings.
* Misunderstanding technical or unclear words.
* Stress, tiredness, or personal thoughts affecting attention.
* Interrupting the speaker before they finish talking.
* Background noise making it difficult to hear properly.

---
**Question 4**
---
### What can you do to improve your listening?

To improve my listening, i can
* Give full attention to the speaker and avoid distractions.
* Maintain eye contact and observe body language.
* Take notes during important discussions or meetings.
* Ask questions if something is not clear.
* Avoid interrupting and let the speaker finish completely.
* Repeat or summarize the message to confirm understanding.
* Practice patience and stay focused during conversations.
* Improve communication and technical vocabulary regularly.

**Question 5**
---
### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

I switch to a passive communication style when I want to avoid conflict or arguments. Sometimes I stay quiet in meetings, avoid sharing my opinion, or agree with others even when I have a different idea. I also become passive when talking to senior people or when I feel less confident.

---
**Question 6**
---
### When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

I may become passive-aggressive when I am upset but do not express my feelings directly. For example, I may give short replies, use sarcasm, avoid conversations, or stay silent when I am unhappy with someone’s behavior. This usually happens when I do not want direct confrontation.

---
**Question 8**
---
### How can you make your communication assertive?

To make my communication more assertive, I can:
* Speak clearly and confidently without being rude.
* Express my thoughts and feelings honestly and respectfully.
* Listen carefully to others before responding.
* Maintain eye contact and positive body language.
* Use calm words instead of emotional reactions.
* Say “no” politely when necessary
* Focus on solving problems instead of blaming others.
* Practice communication regularly in meetings and daily conversations.
* Think before speaking, especially during stressful situations.
* Respect both my opinions and other people’s opinions equally.
