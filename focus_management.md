# Focus Management

## Question 1
### What is Deep Work?

- Deep work means doing work with full concentration and no interruptions  
- It helps in understanding difficult concepts more quickly  
- It increases productivity and improves work quality  
- You can finish important tasks in less time  
- It strengthens your ability to focus for longer periods  
- It supports better performance in learning and professional tasks  

---

## Question 2
### According to the author, how can deep work be practiced effectively?

- Choose a quiet place with fewer distractions  
- Give complete attention to a single task  
- Allocate specific hours for focused work every day  
- Stay away from unnecessary notifications and social media  
- Take short breaks to refresh your mind  
- Maintain a consistent routine for concentration  

---

## Question 3
### How can you apply these principles in daily life?

- Keep a fixed schedule for studying or coding practice  
- Silence mobile notifications while working  
- Use methods such as the Pomodoro technique for better focus  
- Avoid switching between multiple tasks frequently  
- Build the habit of focused practice every day  
- Review and monitor your daily progress regularly

## Question 4

### Dangers of Social Media

- Social media can decrease concentration levels  
- It often leads to distractions and excessive usage habits  
- A lot of productive time gets wasted scrolling online  
- It may create stress through constant comparison with others  
- Overuse can increase anxiety and mental pressure  
- Excessive screen time can disturb sleep and reduce focus
