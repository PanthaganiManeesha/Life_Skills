# Learning Process Review

## Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

**Answer:**  
The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a concept by explaining it in simple words as if teaching someone else.

## Question 3

**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

**Answer:**  

- **Active (Focused) Mode:**  
  When we concentrate deeply on solving a problem or learning something specific.

- **Diffused Mode:**  
  A relaxed thinking mode where the brain makes broader connections and helps in creativity and understanding.
