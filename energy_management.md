# 3. Meditation and Sleep

### Question 4
#### Paraphrase the “Sleep is Your Superpower” video in brief (only points)
- Sleep strengthens memory and learning ability  
- Proper sleep improves focus and thinking power  
- Sleep supports both body and mind recovery  
- Lack of sleep can increase stress and health issues  
- Good sleep helps maintain emotional balance  

---

### Question 5
#### What are some methods you can follow to improve sleep?
- Sleep and wake up at a regular time daily  
- Avoid using mobile phones before bedtime  
- Keep the room calm, dark, and comfortable  
- Practice meditation or deep breathing before sleeping  
- Reduce caffeine intake during nighttime
