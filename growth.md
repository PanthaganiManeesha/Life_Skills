# Growth Mindset and Grit


### Question 1
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

### Answer
Grit means having passion and consistency towards long-term goals. The video explains that success is not only based on talent, but also on continuous effort, patience, and the ability to keep going even after failures.

### Question 2
Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

### Answer
A growth mindset means believing that skills and intelligence can improve through learning and practice. The video explains that mistakes and challenges are opportunities to grow instead of reasons to give up.

---

### Question 3
What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

### Answer
Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions, and efforts control our success and failures rather than luck or other people.

#### Key Point of the Video
The main idea is to focus on things we can control instead of wasting energy on things outside our control. Taking responsibility for our actions helps us stay motivated and improve continuously.
