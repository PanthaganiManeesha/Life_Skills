# Good Practices for Software Development

## Question 1

### What were the new learnings for you?

* One important thing I learned is that clear and regular communication is necessary in a team. Informing teammates early about issues or delays helps avoid confusion and keeps everyone aligned.
* I understood that requirement gathering is more effective when discussions are properly documented and feedback is collected continuously.
* Using productivity and focus tools to reduce distractions was a new concept for me. Applications that block social media or unnecessary websites can help improve concentration.
* Time management tools are useful because they help track daily activities and identify where time is being wasted.
* I also realized that maintaining good health through proper food, sleep, and exercise can positively affect focus, energy, and work performance.

## Question 2

### Which area would you like to improve, and how will you work on it?

* **Improving focus:** I want to reduce distractions while working by keeping my phone away and turning off unnecessary notifications.
* **Better time management:** I plan to divide my work into fixed time slots and concentrate on one task at a time.
* **Creating a productive workspace:** I will choose a clean and quiet environment that helps me stay focused for longer periods.
* **Using productivity tools:** I would like to try tools that block distracting websites and help maintain concentration during work hours.
* **Taking proper breaks:** Short breaks between tasks can help refresh the mind and improve productivity.
* **Tracking daily activities:** I will monitor how I spend my time so that I can improve my efficiency step by step.
* **Continuous improvement:** I want to experiment with different study and work methods to find the approach that works best for me.
