# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

The Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Act is designed to ensure that every employee works in a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment. The law applies to both private and public organizations and aims to prevent inappropriate behavior at the workplace while providing a proper system to address complaints.

---

## Understanding Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment refers to any unwelcome act, comment, gesture, or behavior of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe, humiliated, or disrespected. Such behavior can affect anyone, irrespective of gender.

Examples may include:

* Unwanted physical contact or advances
* Offensive comments, jokes, or messages
* Sharing inappropriate content
* Repeated requests for personal interaction despite refusal
* Any conduct that creates a hostile work environment

---

## Ways to Prevent Sexual Harassment at Work

* Establish and communicate a strong POSH policy across the organization
* Organize regular awareness sessions and employee training programs
* Promote a culture where employees feel comfortable reporting concerns
* Ensure complaints are handled fairly, confidentially, and without bias
* Take immediate action against inappropriate conduct
* Periodically review workplace policies and strengthen preventive measures
